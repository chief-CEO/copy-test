FAQ

What does Scalogy actually do?

Scalogy gives you a private dev server paired with Claude. Describe what you want — a customer dashboard, a scheduled workflow, a branded landing page — and Claude builds and deploys it on your server. No engineering team required.

How does the Claude connector work?

After signup, you add Scalogy as a "connector" in Claude.ai. From there you just talk to Claude in plain English — "build me a client dashboard" or "post to LinkedIn every Tuesday" — and it ships.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel from your dashboard or email us. Everything you built stops running at the end of your billing period.
